# Reproduction report

## Results

The five-seed reduced constructive experiment reports mean held-out L2 errors
of 0.3071274077, 0.2002687395, and 0.0936669784 at widths 16, 64, and 256.
The corresponding label-permutation controls are 0.4622693141, 0.4713274960,
and 0.4757207771, with degradation in all 15 seed-by-width cells.

The retained triangular-tent witness for the Claim 2 proof route has
Linfinity 1.0 and analytic L2 0.0912870929. Dense quadrature differs by
1.46e-10 at the witness width.

## Interpretation

Claim 1 is useful reduced empirical evidence but remains toy because it covers
one target and one explicit encoder/readout, not every bounded Hölder function or
the paper's block-count construction. Claim 2's result falsifies the printed
L2-to-Linfinity inference used in the pinned proof route; it does not settle
all possible Transformer lower-bound arguments.

Claims 3 and 4 were not attempted. No challenge points are claimed.
